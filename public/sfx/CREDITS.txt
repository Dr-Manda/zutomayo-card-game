Zutomayo Card Game — SFX Credits
================================

All source sounds were downloaded from Freesound.org. Each clip below was
processed through a cassette character chain (highpass 80Hz, lowpass 8kHz,
soft compression, 4Hz vibrato) to give it a worn analog quality.

Processing chain (ffmpeg)
-------------------------
  highpass=f=80, lowpass=f=8000,
  acompressor=threshold=-15dB:ratio=4:attack=10:release=200,
  vibrato=f=4:d=0.02

Pitched variants additionally use:
  asetrate=44100*0.84, aresample=44100 (-3 semitones, slower)
  + atempo=1.19 to restore original duration where noted (damage-tick)
